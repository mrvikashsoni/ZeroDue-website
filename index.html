Full Transparency

Device Permissions & Privacy Commitments

We believe in absolute transparency. Here is every Android permission ZeroDue uses and exactly why.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Required on Android 13+ to deliver heads-up recurring bill alerts and post-payment prompts.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Ensures monthly reminders trigger at the precise scheduled time on statement dates and due dates.
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT
Enables fast, secure app unlocking with your fingerprint or face recognition sensor.
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE
Optional toggle: Listens for completed payment alerts locally to send you the 2-minute CC spend prompt.
Zero Internet Permission in Manifest

ZeroDue does not request android.permission.INTERNET. It is physically impossible for the app to send your data over the web or connect to any remote server.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data stored safely?
Yes, 100%. All your credit card spends, reminder schedules, and settings are saved in a local SQLite database directly on your device. ZeroDue does not connect to the internet, and your PIN is secured with hardware-backed encryption.
How does the 2-minute post-payment prompt work?
ZeroDue includes a lightweight Android Notification Listener that detects payment confirmation notifications from popular payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED). When a payment is detected, it starts a local 2-minute timer on your phone and then presents a heads-up popup asking if you made a credit card payment.
What if I forget my 4-digit PIN?
If you have enabled Biometric authentication (Fingerprint or Face ID), you can unlock the app using your biometric sensor. For security reasons in an offline app, if both PIN and biometrics are unavailable, you would need to reinstall the app.
Can I test if reminder notifications work on my device?
Yes! Every bill reminder card has a "Test Alert 🔔" button. Tapping it triggers an instant heads-up notification with sound and vibration so you can verify that your phone's notification settings are configured properly.
